# M7 contract inventory

The T00 inventory of every record, API, tool generation, adapter and protocol
M7 changes, at revision `719b96bb`. Each row names its current status and the
test or file that proves it. It is a record, not a decision: the accepted
[plan](../plans/M7.md) and ADRs own the contracts, and the
[closure runs](M7-closure-runs.md) page owns closure evidence. Part of the
[evidence index](README.md).

Paths are repository-relative. A test is cited as `file` # "name". Digests are
SHA-256 values pinned literally where cited; a row without a digest is proved
by its decoder and replay tests instead.

## Family joins to payloads, paths and decoders

Each family above joins its exact payload schema to a decoder that refuses
altered or relabeled values, as its cited tests show. The public payload
schemas and their vectors live in `apps/loopex_protocol/priv/vectors/`. The
foreground `/3` and daemon `/4` manifests in
`current-contract-manifests.v1.json` bind every public schema, inventory and
path list to the two contract digests. `current_contract_manifest_test.exs`
re-derives them, and the independent Node client reproduces them in
`clients/node/current-contract-manifest-vectors.mjs`. Private record
families are pinned by their Core vector files
(`apps/loopex/priv/vectors/`) and by the delegation byte fixtures in
`apps/loopex_composition/test/fixtures/delegation/`.
